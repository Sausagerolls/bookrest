# Bookrest

A native audiobook player for [Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org) on
iPhone, iPad, Mac and CarPlay.

Bookrest connects to your own Audiobookshelf server. There is no Bookrest account and
no Bookrest cloud. Your books, progress and login stay between your device and your
server.

## Features

- Stream from your server, or download books for offline listening.
- Listening progress syncs with your server across all your devices.
- CarPlay, lock screen and Control Centre controls.
- Optional immersive theme that takes its colours from the cover.
- Connects over HTTPS or plain HTTP, including servers on your home network.

## Requirements

- An Audiobookshelf server you can sign in to.
- iOS / iPadOS 18 or later, or macOS 15 or later.
