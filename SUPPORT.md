# Bookrest Support

## Get help

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Sausagerolls/bookrest/issues). Please
include:

- your device and OS version,
- the Bookrest version (shown on its App Store page under Version History),
- your Audiobookshelf server version,
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

## Common problems

**I can't connect to my server.**
Enter the address exactly as you use it in a browser, including the port if it has
one — for example `https://books.example.com` or `http://192.168.1.4:13378`. If the
server is on your home network, allow Bookrest to access the local network when
asked, or turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

**My progress from another device hasn't appeared.**
Progress is fetched when the app opens or comes back to the foreground. Pull to
refresh on the Home screen to fetch it now.

**A book won't play.**
Check it plays in the Audiobookshelf web app. If the files are missing on the server,
Bookrest cannot play them. Otherwise, open an issue with the book's file format.

**CarPlay doesn't show Bookrest.**
On your iPhone, open Settings → General → CarPlay, choose your car, and check Bookrest
is in the list of apps.

## About Audiobookshelf

Bookrest needs an Audiobookshelf server. See
[audiobookshelf.org](https://www.audiobookshelf.org) to set one up. Bookrest is not
affiliated with the Audiobookshelf project.
