# Bookrest

A native audiobook player for [Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org) — on
iPhone, iPad, Mac and CarPlay.

Bookrest connects to your own Audiobookshelf server and plays your library with the
polish of a first-party app. There is no Bookrest account and no Bookrest cloud: your
books, progress and login stay between your device and your server.

## Features

- **Stream or download.** Play straight from your server, or download books for
  offline listening. Downloads continue in the background.
- **Picks up where you left off, everywhere.** Listening progress syncs with your
  server, so you can switch between phone, tablet, Mac and the Audiobookshelf web app.
- **CarPlay.** Browse and play your library from the car.
- **Lock screen and Control Centre.** Full Now Playing controls, artwork and
  skip buttons.
- **Immersive theme.** Optionally tint the app with colours from the cover of whatever
  is playing.
- **Works on your network.** Connects over HTTPS or plain HTTP, including servers on
  your home network.

## Requirements

- An Audiobookshelf server you can sign in to.
- iOS / iPadOS 18 or later, or macOS 15 or later.

Bookrest collects no data. It talks only to the server you sign in to. Read the full
[Privacy Policy](https://sausagerolls.github.io/bookrest/privacy).
