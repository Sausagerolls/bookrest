# Bookrest Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 19 September 2026*

Bookrest does not collect, store or share any of your data. The developer receives
nothing from the app.

## What the app sends, and where

Bookrest communicates only with the Audiobookshelf server you sign in to. That server
is run by you or by someone you trust — not by Bookrest. To work, the app sends it:

- your username and password when you sign in,
- your listening progress and playback sessions,
- a random device identifier created on install, the app version, your device model
  (for example "iPhone16,2") and OS version, so the server can list the device.

How your server handles this data is governed by whoever runs it.

## What stays on your device

- Your login tokens, stored in the system Keychain.
- Downloaded books and cover art.
- Your settings, the random device identifier and your local listening position.

Deleting the app removes all of this.

## What Bookrest does not do

- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No third-party SDKs.
- No account with the developer.

If you opt in to share analytics with Apple, Apple may provide the developer with
anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's own privacy policy.

## Children

Bookrest is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
